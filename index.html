How it works

Three steps, one afternoon

Your booking system stays. This sits on top of your training program and gives every player a reason to come back next week.

1

Your club signs up

Pick the plan that fits how many players you train. Your state is locked to you while you're with us.

2

Coaches rate the players

From the court, on a phone. Check players in, score a shot on a slider, post a drill for home.

3

Players check their Vitals

Each player logs in to their own stats: level, skill ratings, attendance, coach notes, points.

Inside the app

What your players open

Installs to the home screen like a normal app. No app store, no download, works when the court wifi doesn't.

Skill ratings

Bandeja, víbora, wall exits, volleys, positioning. Each scored out of 10 by the coach, with the change since last time.

Check the group in, rate a shot, post a drill. Everything the coach saves lands in the player's app straight away.

Why clubs sign up

What it does for your club

Players stay longer

When a player can see their level going up, they don't drift off after two months. Progress they can see is progress they pay for.

Your coaches look like pros

Every lesson leaves a record: a score, a note, a drill. Parents and players see the work behind the price.

Nobody else in your state has it

One club per state. Your competition across town can't sign up while you're with us.

More sales at the bar and shop

Points bring players to the counter. Set the rewards yourself: a drink, a grip, club merch.
