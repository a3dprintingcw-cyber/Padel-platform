Common questions

Before you ask

Do my players pay anything?

No. Player accounts are included. Keep charging for lessons the way you do now.

What counts as a training player?

Anyone a coach checks in at least once in a month. Players who only book courts don't count.

Does it replace my booking system?

No. Keep Playtomic, CourtReserve or whatever you use. This handles the training side: ratings, progress, drills, rewards.

What does one club per state mean?

Once your club signs, no other club in your state can get the platform while you're with us. Leave, and the state opens again.

What if we grow past our plan?

You move up at the next billing date. We never cut off a player mid month.

How long does setup take?

An afternoon. Send us your player list and training groups, and your coaches can rate players that same week.

Claim your state

Tell us about your club. We'll set up the app with your name, your coaches and your players, and show it to you before you pay.
