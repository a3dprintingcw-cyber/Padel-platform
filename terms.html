The service

Padel Vitals gives padel clubs an app where coaches record their players' training and players see their own progress. It is run by A3D Printing, Curaçao (a3dprinting.cw@gmail.com).

Clubs

Clubs pay a monthly or yearly fee for their plan, as shown on the pricing page or agreed in writing. A club's city or region is exclusive to it while its subscription is active. Either side can end the subscription with 30 days notice. Clubs are responsible for the people they add and for having their consent.

Players and coaches

Use your own account, keep your password to yourself, and use the app for training at your club. Do not try to see other people's data or get points you did not earn.

Points and rewards

Points have no cash value. Rewards are offered by each club, and the club decides its menu and rules.

Availability

We work to keep the app running and your data safe, but we cannot promise it will never be unavailable. We are not liable for indirect losses from using the app.

Changes

We may update these terms. If a change matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
